# Finding your way

Piano Practice has one list, six rows at its top, and four keys you need
to get around: the jog, its click, Back and Menu. This page walks through
them in the order you meet them, from the first time you open the module.
Drums Practice works the same way, so what you learn here carries over.

## The first time: the welcome

The first time you open Piano Practice, before you have played anything,
four short pages greet you. Turn the **jog** to page through them, or
**click** to go on.

1. **Welcome.** *Piano Practice takes you from your first note to pro,
   one step a day.*
2. **Welcome.** *Lit pads can be pressed.*
3. **Keys.** The five things to remember:

   | Key | Does |
   | --- | --- |
   | **JOG** | move |
   | **CLICK** | open |
   | **BACK** | up one level |
   | **MENU** | help, anywhere |
   | **SHIFT+CLICK** | options |

4. **New to piano?** Turn the jog to your answer and click:
   - **From the start**: the list opens with the cursor on **Continue**,
     which opens your very first step.
   - **I already play**: the **Path** opens, with a tip: *Know a stage
     already? SHIFT+CLICK a stage, then Test out: checkpoint. Pass it and
     the path moves on.* See [The options of a row](#the-options-of-a-row).

**Back** on any page closes the welcome. It does not come back by itself;
**Settings › Help › Welcome again** shows it whenever you like. If you
used Piano Practice before this version, your progress is kept and the
welcome is skipped.

## The top of the list

```
PIANO                   the top of the list
Continue   1.1          your next step
Path       >            five stages, a few weeks each
Practice   >            the free library
Quiz       >            Ear, Reading, Keys
Progress   12%          how far you have got
Settings   >            in groups
```

**Click** opens a row: a folder (`>`) shows what is inside, anything else
opens straight away. **Back** goes up one folder; Back at the top leaves
the module.

- **Continue** names the step it will open, and opens it with one click.
  A name too long for the row shows as its unit's number (`1.1`); with
  the cursor on Continue, the title bar spells it out, `NOTES BY ALPHABET`.
  When a review is due, the row reads **Review** and names the piece that
  is due. Reviews and new steps take turns: one review, then one new step.
- **Path** is the course, in five stages. Each stage lists its units in
  the order a lesson runs (find your way, hear it, learn the pattern, use
  it, show it in a piece) and ends with its **Checkpoint**, the stage's
  exam. Below the stages sits **Review** while anything is due. Every
  exercise on the path is in [the curriculum](curriculum.md).
- **Practice** is everything to play freely: Scales, Chords,
  Progressions, Random, and **Songs** (Classics, Electronic, Techno,
  Styles, and Other for files you added).
- **Quiz** is short timed rounds, by skill. **Ear**: name what you hear.
  **Reading**: read it off the staff. **Keys**: find a named note or
  chord on the pads, or name the pad that lights.
- **Progress** charts how far you have got, overall, per stage, and for
  everything you have played.
- **Settings** is a folder of groups: **Path** (where Continue starts),
  **Practice**, **Sound**, **Pads**, **MIDI**, **Help**. Open a group to
  change its rows: click a row, turn the jog, click again.

The Path has five **stages**, a few weeks each. Every exercise is played
two ways: **Guided**, the pad you need lights up, and **Solo**, nothing
lights up and you read it from the staff. The ready screen shows which one
is armed; the jog changes it.

## The map

**Path** opens as a map: the world, an island for each of the five
stages. Open a stage and you are on its island: a place for each unit, in
the order the lessons run, and at the end a **fort**, the stage's
checkpoint. Your avatar stands where Continue would take you.

The pads are the map: each **lit pad is a place**, right under where it
is drawn, and dark pads are water. On a map the pads make no sound.

| Key | On the map |
| --- | --- |
| **PAD** | walk there; press it again to go in |
| **JOG** | walk to the next place, or back |
| **CLICK** | go in |
| **BACK** | up a level: from an island to the world, the avatar on the island you left |
| **PLAY** | Continue: your next step, from wherever you stand |
| **MENU** | help: the map, then the place under the avatar (a stage's promise, a unit's purpose) |
| **SHIFT + CLICK** | the place's options, and **Show as list** |

The title bar says where you stand and how far you have got there, for
example `1.3 Major scale 40%`, and the reviews due there when they fit.
A place fills as you go; a flag means done, a dotted outline skipped or
not yet ready (it still opens: nothing is locked).

The pads' colours say the same: green done, pulsing white where Continue
is going, dim yellow started, blue skipped, dim the rest, and the brightest
pad the one you stand on.

**Show as list** (in the options) turns the map back into the list for
this session: Review, the folder of what is due, lives in the Path's list.
**Show as map**, or Back, returns to the map.

## The hint line

The bottom line of every screen says which keys work there, the key in
capitals and then what it does:

```
CLICK open  MENU help
```

When a hint is longer than the line it shows in parts that take turns
every three seconds, for example `REC start`, then `PLAY listen`, then
`Guided: pads lit  JOG Solo` on the ready screen.

**Hold SHIFT** and the line shows what Shift does on this screen instead:
`SHIFT: CLICK options` in the list, `SHIFT: CLICK settings` on a piece,
`SHIFT: CLICK skip` on a result, and everywhere `SHIFT: BACK quit`.

## MENU: help, anywhere

Press **MENU** on any screen for its help page:

1. first, **what the screen is**;
2. then its **keys**, the same words as the hint line, Shift included;
3. then, under **More help**, what the page knows about the row or piece
   in front of you: on a Path unit its purpose, on a step what you do and
   why it is there, on a chord lesson its formula; and the words it uses.

The jog scrolls, **Back** or **MENU** closes it, and you are back where
you were. A running piece pauses first, and a quiz round's clock stops
while you read.

## The options of a row

**SHIFT + CLICK** on a row of the list opens its **options**: a short list
of what you can do with that row besides opening it. Only the options
that fit the row are offered, always in this order:

| Option | Offered on | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I know this: skip** | a step, or Continue | passes it by; Continue moves on to the step after. After Guided it counts the step as finished at Guided |
| **I know it all: skip** | a stage, a unit, an exercise of several steps | skips everything inside |
| **Undo skip** | a skipped step, or a folder with skipped steps | takes the skips back |
| **Start the path here** | a stage | Continue starts at this stage; the stages before it count as known |
| **Test out: checkpoint** | a stage with a checkpoint | plays its exam Solo. Pass every step and the path moves on to the next stage |
| **Settings...** | every row | opens Settings |

**Turn the jog** to choose, **click** to do it, **Back** to close the
options without doing anything.

### Example: you already play

1. Open **Path**. The cursor is on **1 Grid and staff**.
2. **SHIFT + CLICK**, turn to **Test out: checkpoint**, click.
3. The first checkpoint step opens Solo. Play it through; each pass
   opens the next one.
4. Pass them all and the screen says *Checkpoint 1 passed. The path moves
   on.* Continue now starts in stage 2. The steps you skipped stay open,
   marked as assumed: play them any time.

## First-visit tips

The first time you see some screens, a one-page tip explains them:

- the options of a row: *Options for this row. JOG choose, CLICK do it,
  BACK close.*
- your first piece: *REC plays and scores it, PLAY lets you listen first.
  MENU: help.*
- your first quiz: *Answer with the lit pads or the jog. MENU: help.*

**Click** or **Back** closes a tip. Each one shows once.
**Settings › Help › Show tips again** brings them all back.
