# Finding your way

Piano Practice opens on **home**, five circles, and behind each circle is
a list of folders. Four keys get you around: the jog, its click, Back and
Menu. This page walks through them in the order you meet them, from the
first time you open the module. Drums Practice works the same way, so what
you learn here carries over.

## The first time: the welcome

The first time you open Piano Practice, before you have played anything,
four short pages greet you. Turn the **jog** to page through them, or
**click** to go on.

1. **Welcome.** *Piano Practice takes you from your first note to pro,
   one step a day.*
2. **Welcome.** *Lit pads can be pressed.*
3. **Keys.** The five things to remember:

   | Key | Does |
   | --- | --- |
   | **JOG** | move |
   | **CLICK** | open |
   | **BACK** | up one level |
   | **MENU** | help, anywhere |
   | **SHIFT+CLICK** | options |

4. **New to piano?** Turn the jog to your answer and click:
   - **From the start**: the **Learning Path** opens, as its map, your avatar on
     the first stage. **PLAY** opens your very first step.
   - **I already play**: the **Learning Path** opens, with a tip: *Know a stage
     already? SHIFT+CLICK a stage, then Test out: checkpoint. Pass it and
     the path moves on.* See [The options of a row](#the-options-of-a-row).

**Back** on any page closes the welcome. It does not come back by itself;
**Settings › Advanced › Help › Welcome again** shows it whenever you like. If you
used Piano Practice before this version, your progress is kept and the
welcome is skipped.

## Home

```
PIANO
   (o)  (o)  (O)  (o)  (o)       Learning Path, Practice, Songs, Progress, Settings
             Songs
JOG choose  CLICK open
```

Home is five circles, each with its picture: an island for the **Learning Path**,
keys for **Practice**, a note for **Songs**, bars for **Progress**
and a cog for **Settings**. The chosen one is filled and grown, its name
underneath.

| Key | At home |
| --- | --- |
| **JOG** | choose: the next circle grows, the one you left shrinks |
| **CLICK** | open the chosen circle |
| **PAD** | open a circle straight away: the five lit pads of the bottom row, left to right |
| **MENU** | help: home, then what the chosen circle holds |
| **BACK** | leave the module |
| **SHIFT + CLICK** | options, and **Show as list** |

The pads make no sound at home: lit means *press me*, and the chosen
circle's pad pulses. Back from a section lands on home with its circle
chosen, so you are where you left. **Show as list** shows the five as an
ordinary list for this session; Back turns it into circles again.

**Practice** and **Songs** open as rings of their own, the same keys and
the same pads, and so does each trainer in Practice:

| Ring | Circles |
| --- | --- |
| **Practice** | Notes (a note on a line), Intervals (two notes, bracketed), Scales (stairs), Chords (a chord), Progressions (blocks and arrows) |
| **A trainer** | Read (a staff), Hear (an ear), Play (a key with its letter), Learn (a book), Stats (bars) — the same five in every trainer |
| **Songs** | Classics, Electronic, Techno, Styles |

The title bar names the ring, MENU explains it, **Show as list** works
there too, and **Back** goes up with that circle chosen. Deeper folders,
such as a trainer's Learn or a genre of Songs, are lists.

Behind each circle, **click** opens a row: a folder (`>`) shows what is
inside, anything else opens straight away. **Back** goes up one folder,
and from the top of a section to home.

- **Learning Path** is the course, in five stages, drawn as [a map](#the-map). Each
  stage lists its units in the order a lesson runs (find your way, hear it,
  learn the pattern, use it, show it in a piece) and ends with its
  **Checkpoint**, the stage's exam. Every exercise on the path is in
  [the curriculum](curriculum.md).
- **Continue** opens your next step: **PLAY** on the Learning Path's map, from
  wherever you stand. Shown as a list (**Show as list** in the options), the
  Learning Path starts with a **Continue** row that names the step: a name too long
  for the row shows as its unit's number (`1.1`), and with the cursor on it
  the title bar spells it out, `NOTES BY ALPHABET`. When a review is due,
  the row reads **Review** and names the piece that is due. Reviews and new
  steps take turns: one review, then one new step. Below the stages sits
  **Review** while anything is due.
- **Practice** is five trainers: Notes, Intervals, Scales, Chords and
  Progressions. Each is **Read** off the staff, **Hear** by ear, **Play**
  from a name, **Learn** (its lessons) and **Stats** (what you know, weakest
  first). Read, Hear and Play open the knob screen — what a round asks, a
  knob each, the same knob in every trainer, **PLAY** to start — that
  remembers what you set. The walk-through is
  [Practice: five trainers](trainers.md).
- **Songs** is the pieces: Classics, Electronic, Techno, Styles, and Other
  for files you added.
- **Progress** charts how far you have got, overall, per stage, and for
  everything you have played.
- **Settings** is one short page of what is yours: **Octave**, **Click**,
  **Key**, **Scale** and **Start at** (where Continue starts). Click a row,
  turn the jog, click again. Its last row, **Advanced**, opens the rest in
  groups: **Practice**, **Sound**, **MIDI** and **Help**. Back goes back a
  step at a time. On a piece, **SHIFT + CLICK** opens the page, and again
  on any settings page goes straight back to the piece.

The Learning Path has five **stages**, a few weeks each. Every exercise is played
two ways: **Guided**, the pad you need lights up, and **Solo**, nothing
lights up and you read it from the staff. The ready screen shows which one
is armed; the jog changes it.

## The map

**Learning Path** opens as a map: the world, an island for each of the five
stages. Open a stage and you are on its island: a place for each unit, in
the order the lessons run, and at the end a **fort**, the stage's
checkpoint. Your avatar stands where Continue would take you.

The pads are the map: each **lit pad is a place**, right under where it
is drawn, and dark pads are water. On a map the pads make no sound.

| Key | On the map |
| --- | --- |
| **PAD** | walk there; press it again to go in |
| **JOG** | walk to the next place, or back |
| **CLICK** | go in |
| **BACK** | up a level: from an island to the world, the avatar on the island you left |
| **PLAY** | Continue: your next step, from wherever you stand |
| **MENU** | help: the map, then the place under the avatar (a stage's promise, a unit's purpose) |
| **SHIFT + CLICK** | the place's options, and **Show as list** |

The title bar says where you stand and how far you have got there, for
example `1.3 Major scale 40%`, and the reviews due there when they fit.
A place fills as you go; a flag means done, a dotted outline skipped or
not yet ready (it still opens: nothing is locked).

The pads' colours say the same: green done, pulsing white where Continue
is going, dim yellow started, blue skipped, dim the rest, and the brightest
pad the one you stand on.

### The way moves with you

The map shows where you are going as you go:

- **Into an island**: a click on a stage zooms in to its island, and
  **Back** zooms out to the world, your avatar on the island you left.
- **A place done**: when you finish the last step of a place, **Next**
  first raises its flag, then walks your avatar to the place of your next
  step, and opens it.
- **A stage done**, or its checkpoint passed: fireworks over the stage,
  out to the world, the sail to the next island, and in to the place
  where you go on.
- **The whole Learning Path done**: the finale on the world map.
- **A level learned** the first time: a star round your score on the
  result.

The bottom line says `ANY KEY skip`: any key or pad skips the scene and
goes straight to where it was going. Nothing is played or scored while a
scene runs.

**Show as list** (in the options) turns the map back into the list for
this session: Review, the folder of what is due, lives in the Learning Path's list.
**Show as map**, or Back, returns to the map.

## Levels, on the ready screen

A song has up to four levels: **L1 RH melody**, **L1 LH bass**, **L2 RH
chords** and **L3 Both hands**. A song is one row of the list: open it and
you are on its ready screen at once, on the first level you have not
learned yet.

```
Ode to Joy        Piano all
+--------------+-----------+
| > listen     |   LEVEL   |
| o practise   |  <  2 G > |
| @ scrub      |           |
|--------------+-----------|
| Knob 8:  72 bpm          |
+--------------------------+
L2 RH chords  Guided
```

The **LEVEL** box shows the level as a big digit, **G** or **S** for
Guided or Solo beside it, arrows where the jog can go on, and a tick
once that level is learned. **Turn the jog** to walk the levels and their
two ways together: 1 Guided, 1 Solo, 2 Guided, 2 Solo, and so on, stopping
at both ends. A new level starts from bar 1. The bottom line names it, and
takes turns with the keys.

Under them the box names **knob 8** and the tempo it sets, live as you turn
it. A drill without levels has the same row. On a song knob 8 slows (or
speeds) the song itself, for as long as it stays open: learn it slowly,
then build it up. Opened again, it starts at its written tempo.

A step of the Learning Path, Continue and Review open the level they are about.

## The hint line

The bottom line of every screen says which keys work there, the key in
capitals and then what it does:

```
CLICK open  MENU help
```

When a hint is longer than the line it shows in parts that take turns
every three seconds, for example `REC start`, then `PLAY listen`, then
`Guided: pads lit  JOG Solo` on the ready screen.

**Hold SHIFT** and the line shows what Shift does on this screen instead:
`SHIFT: CLICK options` in the list, `SHIFT: CLICK settings` on a piece,
`SHIFT: CLICK skip` on a result, and everywhere `SHIFT: BACK quit`.

## MENU: help, anywhere

Press **MENU** on any screen for its help page:

1. first, **what the screen is**;
2. then its **keys**, the same words as the hint line, Shift included;
3. then, under **More help**, what the page knows about the row or piece
   in front of you: on a Learning Path unit its purpose, on a step what you do and
   why it is there, on a chord lesson its formula; and the words it uses.

The jog scrolls, **Back** or **MENU** closes it, and you are back where
you were. A running piece pauses first, and a quiz round's clock stops
while you read.

## The options of a row

**SHIFT + CLICK** on a row of the list opens its **options**: a short list
of what you can do with that row besides opening it. Only the options
that fit the row are offered, always in this order:

| Option | Offered on | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I know this: skip** | a step, or Continue | passes it by; Continue moves on to the step after. After Guided it counts the step as finished at Guided |
| **I know it all: skip** | a stage, a unit, an exercise of several steps | skips everything inside |
| **Undo skip** | a skipped step, or a folder with skipped steps | takes the skips back |
| **Start the path here** | a stage | Continue starts at this stage; the stages before it count as known |
| **Test out: checkpoint** | a stage with a checkpoint | plays its exam Solo. Pass every step and the path moves on to the next stage |
| **Show as list** | home, or a map | the same rows as an ordinary list, for this session |
| **Show as map** | a map shown as a list | the map again (Back does the same) |
| **Settings...** | every row | opens the Settings page |

**Turn the jog** to choose, **click** to do it, **Back** to close the
options without doing anything.

### Example: you already play

1. Open **Learning Path**. The cursor is on **1 Grid and staff**.
2. **SHIFT + CLICK**, turn to **Test out: checkpoint**, click.
3. The first checkpoint step opens Solo. Play it through; each pass
   opens the next one.
4. Pass them all and the screen says *Checkpoint 1 passed. The path moves
   on.* Continue now starts in stage 2. The steps you skipped stay open,
   marked as assumed: play them any time.

## First-visit tips

The first time you see some screens, a one-page tip explains them:

- the options of a row: *Options for this row. JOG choose, CLICK do it,
  BACK close.*
- your first piece: *REC plays and scores it, PLAY lets you listen first.
  MENU: help.*
- your first quiz: *Answer with the lit pads or the jog. MENU: help.*

**Click** or **Back** closes a tip. Each one shows once.
**Settings › Advanced › Help › Show tips again** brings them all back.
